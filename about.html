01 The path

India to Berlin, the long way round

Six moves, none of them obvious at the time.

01
Roots in western India

A small city where people take pride in doing things by hand. No design studios around me, but craft and community, and that instinct never left.

02
Chasing the image

After my tenth I left for Mumbai to study animation, drawn to how a single frame could hold emotion. It taught me to care how something makes people feel, not just how it looks.

03
Learning widely, on purpose

Alongside the creative training I finished a Master's in English, in parallel with a business degree. Art, logic and language, which turns out to be the job description.

04
Four years in post-production

I loved the craft and felt my growth slowing. There was a whole side of making I had not touched. Uncomfortable to admit, and the reason anything changed.

05
Finding UX through people

With almost no UX courses near me I found one online, the Interaction Design Foundation. Understanding why people do what they do clicked immediately. Less a career switch than a homecoming.

06
Where I am now

Certified User Analyst from HFI in 2024, and a move to Germany to take this further. A designer shaped by animation and hands-on craft, working in Berlin.
