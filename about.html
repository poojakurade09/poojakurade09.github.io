The Story

My Journey to Design

01

Roots in Kolhapur, India

I was born in Kolhapur, a small city in Maharashtra, India, where people take pride in doing things by hand. I didn't grow up around design studios, but around craft and community, and that instinct has never left me.

02

Chasing the Image

After my 10th, I left for Mumbai to study animation, drawn to how a single frame could hold emotion. That leap taught me to chase what I love, and to care about how something makes people feel, not just how it looks.

03

Learning Widely

I've never liked staying in one lane. Alongside my creative training, I completed a degree in Business Economics and a Master's in English, in parallel. Looking back, I was quietly assembling the exact toolkit a designer needs: art, logic, and empathy.

04

Four Years in Post-Production

I spent four years in post-production and loved the craft, but felt my growth slowing down. There was a whole side of making I hadn't touched yet. That restlessness was uncomfortable, but honest, and it pushed me toward what came next.

05

Finding UX Through People

With almost no UX courses around me, I found one online: the Interaction Design Foundation. Everything clicked there, understanding why people do what they do. UX wasn't a career switch so much as a homecoming.

06

Where I Am Now

In 2024, I earned my Certified User Analyst (CUA) certification from HFI. Today I'm a UX designer shaped by animation, economics, and hands-on craft. After everything this journey has taught me, I moved to Germany to push my career to its next level.

How I Work

Design Process

Rooted in empathy, curiosity, and iteration, my process turns real user insights into clear, intuitive solutions that people enjoy using and that move the business forward.

Discover
Every project starts with listening. Through research, audits, and honest conversation, I uncover the real goals, user needs, and friction points hiding beneath the surface.
Design
From insight to interface, I shape ideas into wireframes, prototypes, and polished visuals, making every decision with clarity, intention, and close collaboration.
Deliver
Focused and launch-ready. I hand off scalable, well-documented design and stay close through build, so good ideas actually make it to real people.
