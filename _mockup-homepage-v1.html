Designing with AI

AI is part of how I think, not just what I ship.

I use AI the way I'd use a sharp collaborator: to move faster through research, stress test ideas earlier, and spend more of my time on the judgment calls that actually need a designer. Here's where it shows up in my process.

01
Research
Synthesizing interviews and competitive scans in hours, not days, so I can spend that time talking to more real users.
02
Ideation
Using AI to generate rougher, weirder first drafts I can react to, instead of staring at a blank page.
03
Prototyping
Going from idea to a clickable, real feeling prototype in the same afternoon I had the idea.
04
Problem solving
Treating AI as a second opinion on tradeoffs, then making the final call myself.
